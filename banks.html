For banks

Put your card back at the moment of payment.

MerchantLink gives your cardholders thousands of local merchants you have no relationship with today, and gives your card a reason to be the one that pays, whether the shopper or their agent completes the purchase.

What you get

Give points an everyday use

78% of Canadians want more flexible redemption options.1 Only 22% of credit card customers redeem for travel and entertainment, down from 26% in 2023.2 36% redeemed rewards for essentials in the past year.3 MerchantLink puts redemption at the dry cleaner, the bakery, the hardware store.

See the basket

When your card or account pays, you see what was bought, line by line, and whether points or the cardholder's own funds paid. With the shopper's consent. Card rails carry the merchant and the amount. MerchantLink adds the basket.

Join an open network

Discovery is shared by every participating bank. Your edge is at the moment of payment, where your card or account closes the sale and earns the data.

Use the clean room

You see all of your own data, plus blinded aggregates from the network: what sells, where, by category and month. Never another bank's rows. Groups under 10 shoppers stay hidden.

Clean shopping agent execution, on your terms

  • Launch agentic shopping for your cardholders under your rules: which agents, which merchants, which limits.
  • Keep your card on top of the wallet, not the card an agent picks by default.
  • Every agent is declared and verified. Every purchase traces back to the cardholder who authorized it.

Open banking catalyst

  • Clean retail offerings straight from each merchant's own POS or e-commerce system, not scraped from the web.
  • Verified merchants, consented data and line-level baskets ready the day consumer-driven banking opens consented data sharing.
  • One connection to thousands of local merchants, instead of one integration per provider.
  • Consent records and data minimization built in from the start.

What it does not need

No new app. No POS change. No open banking dependency.

  • Works on existing card-linked rails and provider APIs.
  • Consumer-driven banking, when it arrives, speeds up consented data sharing. Nothing waits for it.
  • Proposed pilot: one neighbourhood, one program, 90 days.
