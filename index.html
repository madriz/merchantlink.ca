The problems

Countless blind spots in merchant and customer relationships.

Canadian small merchants run on dozens of POS, e-commerce and payment providers. Each one sees a slice of the catalog.

Bank points pile up with few everyday places to spend them.

Agents index the large retailers and miss the shop down the street.

How it works

Four simple steps.

01 Connect

The merchant links their POS or payment provider once. We build to every major provider, not one.

02 Verify

Every merchant completes business verification (KYB). Banks and agents only see legitimate merchants.

03 Harden

We help merchants expose clean catalogs so agents can read them: names, identifiers, sizes, stock, images.

04 Open

The catalog is open to participating banks and to any declared, verified agent.

Who pays

Merchants keep full control of their prices and receive the full card payment. The shopper pays with their own money, by credit or debit, or with points their bank already owes them.

For banks

  • See the full basket when your card pays.
  • Increase the utility and reach of your loyalty programs.
  • Join an open clean-room network where your edge is the moment of payment.
  • Improve your risk management tools.

For merchants

  • Scale the reach of your catalog.
  • Receive points redemptions at face value from participating banks.
  • Better intelligence on your market and your customers.
  • Make your catalog ready for shopping agents.

For shoppers and agents

  • Spend points at local shops.
  • Pay by debit or credit. Use the agent of your choice on the same verified catalog.
  • Control what data you share, and with whom.
