Privacy

What this site collects.

Last updated: Nov 19, 2026.

About this site

MerchantLink is a framework by Rodrigo Madriz. It is not an operating business. Nothing is bought or sold here.

Usage data, only if you accept

With your consent, merchantlink.ca uses Google Analytics 4, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to count visits and see which pages and demo screens are used. Google Analytics records pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, and how you arrived.

Analytics stays off until you choose Accept. If you choose Decline, or make no choice, no analytics cookies are set. Your choice is kept in your browser and you can change it at any time with .

The contact form

When you request a briefing, the form sends your name, organization, role, work email and message to the MerchantLink inbox through Web3Forms, a form delivery service. We use them to reply to you.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • We do not use advertising cookies or show ads.
  • The demo runs on catalog snapshots and test-mode payment calls. It asks for no personal information.

Google Analytics and Web3Forms are third-party services. They may store and process data outside Canada, including in the United States.

How long we keep it

  • Analytics data: 2 months, then Google deletes it.
  • Contact form details: kept only as long as needed to answer you. If no follow-up is needed, they are deleted within 30 days.

Contact

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you sent us. The person in charge of protecting personal information at MerchantLink answers these requests.

To reach them, use the contact form, choose "Other" and mention privacy in your message.
